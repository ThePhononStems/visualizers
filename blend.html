What can I load into a layer?

This projector needs images to blend. For each layer you can load:

  • A Flickr album URL — e.g. flickr.com/photos/name/albums/123 (album pages only, not single-photo pages).
  • Any direct image URL — a link ending in an image file, e.g. https://example.com/photo.jpg.
  • A remote list of direct image URLs — a link to a hosted .txt file with one image URL per line.
  • Your own files — upload .jpg, .png, and other image files straight from your computer with the Upload your own photos button.
